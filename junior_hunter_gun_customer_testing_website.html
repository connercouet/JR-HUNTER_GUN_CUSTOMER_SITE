About the Junior Hunter Gun

A realistic shotgun form factor with smart sensors designed to build good habits safely and enjoyably.

Why it matters

Hands‑on training with instant feedback reduces risk while increasing engagement for young hunters and their coaches.

Who it's for

Parents, youth programs, trap teams, and conservation groups preparing beginners for safe field experiences.

What you can do

Test the prototype, share your feedback, and help shape the next iteration before production.

Key Features

Purpose‑built for waterfowl training with modern tech under the hood.

Barrel‑Mounted Camera

Captures aiming data; trigger snapshot records point‑of‑aim for instant review.

Side Display + Safety Sensors

Video recording when safety is off; photo on trigger pull for coaching moments.

Bluetooth Connectivity

Sync footage to phone or tablet for analysis, sharing, and long‑term progress tracking.

Realistic Ergonomics

Weight and balance tuned to emulate a shotgun for authentic handling.

Coach Review Mode

Instant playback of photos/videos for side‑by‑side instruction and feedback.

Companion App (alpha)

Optional app for session logging, achievements, and safe‑handling metrics.

Customer Testing Program

We’re partnering with parents, instructors, and clubs to validate the design in real training settings. Your experience helps refine ergonomics, UI, and safety prompts.

  • Evaluate setup, handling, and clarity of on‑screen feedback.
  • Try demo drills (mount, swing, aim, safety on/off) and note usability.
  • Share suggestions about durability, grips, and learning features.
